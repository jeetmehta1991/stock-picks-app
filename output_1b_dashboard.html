Key findings & recommendations
Buy-the-dip works in specific windows. Jul 2022 (bear market rally): 56.7% WR, +2.1% avg PnL. Oct 2022 (regime turn): 44.2% WR, +0.66%. Mar 2022 (commodity spike): 42.9% WR, +0.23%. These 3 months validate the core philosophy.
Risk Agent is broken in sustained crisis. Scored exactly 2/10 on all 34,727 trades — zero variance. A constant adds no signal. Cost: ~$150. Value: near zero. Fix needed for Phase 1C: score relative to crisis baseline.
Upgrade threshold was never reachable. Max score observed: 42. Upgrade threshold: 75. No trade could ever be upgraded. Thresholds were miscalibrated for crisis regime before the run.
Trade inflation 3.5x overstates sample sizes. 34,727 rows = ~9,900 independent decisions. Multiple strategies on same ticker+date inflate counts. The 500-trade minimum = effectively 143 independent positions. Needs recalibration.
Best sectors in crisis: Utilities (36.3% WR) and Health Care (32.0%). Defensive sectors significantly outperform. Phase 1C should weight these higher in crisis regime.
Sectors to block in crisis long strategies: Emerging Markets (0% WR), Volatility ETFs (11.7%), Fixed Income (16.1%). Data-confirmed losers — should be screener-blocked, not left to agents.
Worst trades all hit earnings. TGT -25.5% (May earnings miss), ALGN -22.6%, AKAM -14.1%. All circuit-broken within 8 days. An earnings proximity filter would prevent the worst losses.
Phase 1B re-run recommendation: no agents ($0 cost). Agents added $150 in cost and near-zero differentiation in crisis regime. Run Phase 1B without agents on laptop. Use Sonnet agents in Phase 1C on passing strategies only.
