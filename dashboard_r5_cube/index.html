This dashboard is not for universal strategy verdicts. The cube surfaces best (strategy × exit × regime × ticker × classifier) combinations. A strategy that lost money at universe-wide aggregate may still be optimal for specific regimes / sectors / cap-bands / tiers / volatility bands. No strategy is universally disabled.

Use the Cube Explorer tab to slice across classifiers. Use Survivors for IS+OOS-validated combinations. Use Underperformers for analysis priority. Quiet lists 60 strategies that fired zero trades — investigation findings catalogued in BUILD_PLAN_PROGRESS.md.
