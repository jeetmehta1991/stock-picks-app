Key findings & recommendations for Phase 1C
Buy-the-dip works in specific windows. Jul 2022 (bear market rally): 56.7% WR, +2.1% avg PnL. Oct 2022 (regime turn): 44.2% WR, +0.66%. Mar 2022 (commodity spike): 42.9% WR, +0.23%. These 3 months validate the core philosophy.
Risk Agent is broken in sustained crisis. Scored exactly 2/10 on all 34,727 trades — zero variance across the entire run. A constant adds no signal. Fix needed for Phase 1C: score relative to crisis baseline, not absolute.
Upgrade threshold was never reachable. Max final score observed: 42. Upgrade threshold: 75. The thresholds were miscalibrated for crisis regime. No trade could ever be upgraded in these conditions.
Trade inflation 3.5x overstates sample sizes. 34,727 rows = ~9,900 independent decisions. Multiple strategies on same ticker+date inflate counts. 500-trade minimum = effectively 143 independent positions.
Best sectors in crisis: Utilities (36.3% WR, −0.16% avg) and Health Care (32.0%, −0.48%). Only Utilities had near-breakeven PnL. Defensive sectors significantly outperform cyclicals.
Block these sectors from long strategies in crisis: Emerging Markets (0% WR), Volatility ETFs (11.7%), Fixed Income (16.1%). Data-confirmed systematic losers.
Worst trades all hit earnings. TGT −25.5% (May 2022 earnings miss, 8 days), ALGN −22.6%, AKAM −14.1%. All circuit-broken within 8 days. Earnings proximity filter would prevent worst losses.
Phase 1B re-run: no agents ($0 cost). Agents added $150 and near-zero differentiation. Run Phase 1B without agents on laptop. Use Sonnet in Phase 1C on passing strategies only — that’s the correct use of AI budget.
