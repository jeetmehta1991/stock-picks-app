Phase 1A — 10 passing criteria (all required)
#MetricThresholdWhy it mattersAudit flag
1Win rate≥ 55%Minimum hit rate for a profitable strategy after costsFlag if > 75% — likely overfit
2Profit factor> 1.2Total wins ÷ total losses. 1.2 = for every $1 lost, make $1.20. Professional standard for diversified systems. Above 1.5 flagged for look-ahead bias audit.Flag if > 1.5
3Expected value per trade> 0(Win rate × avg win) + (loss rate × avg loss) must be positive. A strategy can have 60% win rate and still lose money if losses are much bigger than wins.—
4Win/loss ratio> 1.0Average winning trade must be larger than average losing trade. Ensures a single large loss cannot wipe out multiple small wins.—
5Max drawdown< 20%Worst peak-to-trough loss on the equity curve. Beyond 20% most traders psychologically abandon the strategy before it recovers.—
6Total ROI> 0%The strategy must be net profitable over the full 3-year backtest period. Positive win rate with negative total ROI means something is wrong.—
7Smart money liftMeasurable improvementWin rate must be higher when congressional + insider signals align vs when they don't. Validates that smart money data adds predictive value.—
8Macro correlationMeasurable improvementWin rate must be higher in favourable macro regimes (VIX low, yield curve normal) vs unfavourable. Validates the regime filter is working.—
9Minimum trades≥ 100Statistical validity threshold. Below 100 trades, win rate is too noisy to be meaningful. A coin flip can show 70% win rate over 10 flips.—
10Regime coverageProfitable in ≥ 2 of 5 regimesA strategy that only works in bull markets cannot be trusted with real money. Must prove robustness across at least 2 different market conditions.—
